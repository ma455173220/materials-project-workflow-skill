# VASP convergence recovery

Use this only for VASP tasks. Apart from the optimizer default, the options below are diagnoses, not universal INCAR values or permission to start a new scientific branch. Apply them to the project's reviewed route and current evidence. For reaction paths and saddle searches, also read [CI-NEB/IDM guidance](transition-states.md).

Unless the researcher's default or the project chooses otherwise, DFT geometry relaxation, coarse or fine (not CI-NEB or IDM), uses native CG (`IBRION=2`) with the stage's chosen negative `EDIFFG` force criterion. VTST FIRE (`IBRION=3`, `POTIM=0`, `IOPT=7` together) may be chosen only on a verified VTST build: verified means site build information or an earlier successful VTST run on that executable; in the first ionic steps confirm that atoms actually move, because a non-VTST build will not relax the structure with these tags. Check compatibility with the selected cell/constraint setup. With FIRE, switch to `IBRION=2` only when the optimizer itself fails (step 3); a timeout with continuing progress is a continuation (step 4), not a switch. The switch covers that structure's remaining attempts in the current stage and counts as a restart; its next stage starts again with FIRE. If the failure showed that VTST does not support the setup itself, use CG for every structure with that setup. Keep the reviewed first-input process and finite DFT allowance. Final energy comparisons require common energy-affecting settings and accepted geometries, not an automatic switch back to CG.

1. Check the current job's identity and terminal state. Read complete recent ionic steps in `OUTCAR`/`OSZICAR`, electronic convergence, forces, energy trend and geometry; inspect `CONTCAR` and errors. The electronic minimization in the first one or two **ionic** steps of a new geometry relaxation, especially the first, may hit `NELM` without justifying an immediate stop: check whether subsequent ionic steps' SCF, forces, energy and geometry recover. A nonfinite result, implausible structure or explicit fatal error needs separate diagnosis.
2. If later SCF steps keep failing, check geometry, spin initialization and whether the restart wavefunction/charge matches this attempt. Try a justified electronic-solver change such as `ALGO`, mixing, `NELM` or tighter electronic accuracy when force noise warrants it. Record the full input difference and observed response. Do not copy another system's mixing or smearing numbers as defaults.
3. For ordinary geometry relaxation, reliable SCF, reasonable geometry and a useful downward energy trend justify continuing despite moderate force fluctuations; force oscillation alone is not a stop trigger. Diagnose persistent oscillation or stagnation from the combined force, energy and geometry trends across trustworthy ionic steps, rather than exhausting the walltime. If the next steps are unlikely to help, preserve evidence and request a controlled VASP stop with `STOPCAR`/`LSTOP=.TRUE.` at the next ionic step where applicable; confirm that the job actually ended, and remove the stop request before any corrected submission. When the optimizer itself is the problem, a justified change of optimizer counts as a restart (FIRE only on a verified VTST build, as above). Fix SCF, geometry or spin problems directly rather than changing the optimizer for them. Do not mechanically rotate algorithms or call an unchanged-input rerun an algorithm change. Before comparing energies, converge/evaluate each candidate under the project's common final energy-affecting settings; a different successful ionic optimizer alone need not invalidate its geometry.
4. If a valid structure continues to improve when `NSW` or walltime ends, continue autonomously from a verified checkpoint within the approved per-job walltime and remaining restart allowance; timeout alone is not a per-restart approval gate. Use the counting/review rule in `SKILL.md`; do not reset the allowance by changing algorithms or run directories. An incomplete or invalid stage cannot be reported as accepted or used to enter the next scientific stage. Do not repeat the same failed input without a plausible reason and a finite stopping condition.
5. Temporary coarse settings or a different restart source may help reach a workable geometry. Preserve each attempt's original inputs/outputs and the choice to use or omit `WAVECAR`/`CHGCAR`; never delete the source evidence to test a fresh start. A new physical model, magnetic branch or final comparison protocol requires a scientific decision.

All endpoints being compared must satisfy the same project-approved formal relaxation and final-energy protocol, including relevant functional/POTCAR, cutoff, k points, smearing, spin/U and energy definition. Coarse-stage energies are diagnostics, not final rankings. A separate static calculation is appropriate when the chosen protocol requires it.

Distinguish three claims at completion: (a) the scheduler reports the identified job terminal with a successful exit; (b) VASP output is complete, with no fatal error or truncated `OUTCAR`/`OSZICAR`, a valid `CONTCAR` and a consistent final ionic step; (c) the relaxation actually met the selected ionic force/energy criterion and has acceptable late SCF, forces and geometry. Reaching `NSW` or seeing electronic `EDIFF` alone does not prove (c), and a last `OSZICAR` line alone does not prove (a) or (b). For a fully finished, accepted geometry run, extract the last ionic-step `F` and `E0` from `OSZICAR`; use one energy definition consistently within the project's comparison protocol and retain the original file. Do not force an extra static calculation unless the project's final-energy protocol calls for one.

Sources: [Rosen Group's VASP heuristics](https://rosengroup.slite.page/p/PjutyhI3iHVl7c/Assorted-Wisdom), [VASP IBRION documentation](https://vasp.at/wiki/IBRION), [VASP STOPCAR](https://vasp.at/wiki/STOPCAR), [VASP EDIFFG](https://vasp.at/wiki/index.php/EDIFFG), [VASP OSZICAR](https://vasp.at/wiki/OSZICAR), [VTST optimizers](https://henkelmangroup.github.io/vtsttools/optimizers.html). These are suggestions to assess, not fixed thresholds; the Skill does not require another local manual or repository.
